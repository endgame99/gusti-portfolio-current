### Requirement status convention

- `CONFIRMED` — explicitly stated by the owner or directly verified in the canonical repository.
- `CURRENT_STATE` — describes the current implementation; it is not automatically a target-state requirement.
- `INTERPRETATION_REQUIRES_CONFIRMATION` — a structured interpretation of the owner's material that must be approved before being treated as final product truth.
- `MISSING_DATA_REQUIRED` — the decision or evidence does not yet exist. Do not infer or fabricate it.
- `OUT_OF_SCOPE` — intentionally excluded from the current product foundation.

### Authority and factual integrity

When sources conflict, follow this order:

1. The owner's explicit instruction for the current task.
2. Verified code, data, and local assets in the canonical repository.
3. This PRD after owner approval.
4. `docs/UI_GUIDELINES.md` for visual and interaction implementation.
5. A reference explicitly named for a scoped task.

Legacy repositories, old prompts, old PRDs, old blueprints, and award/reference websites are not product truth unless the owner explicitly promotes a specific item into scope.

## 1. Executive product definition

### 1.1 What the owner is trying to build

`INTERPRETATION_REQUIRES_CONFIRMATION`

GUSTI Portfolio is not intended to be a passive personal portfolio or a generic creative CV. It is intended to operate as a commercial authority, proof, and pitching system that improves the owner's position before a sales conversation begins.

The website uses:

- Curated work to prove execution quality.
- Case studies to demonstrate judgment, process, and relevant outcomes.
- Services to connect buyer problems with work GUSTI can complete.
- Library content to demonstrate current knowledge, experimentation, and applied use of new creative technology.
- A clear doctrine to distinguish GUSTI's commercial thinking from tool-led creative vendors.

The intended commercial effect is to increase trust, reputation, project quality, and bargaining power, leading to paid work at the highest value the market and evidence can support.

### 1.2 Product statement

`INTERPRETATION_REQUIRES_CONFIRMATION`

> GUSTI Portfolio is a commercial proof platform for product visuals, ecommerce creative, campaign content, and AI-assisted visual production. It presents finished outcomes, applied judgment, and verified evidence so prospective clients can understand the value of working with GUSTI before entering a project discussion.

This is a product-definition statement, not final homepage copy.

### 1.3 The website's job

`CONFIRMED`

The website must support the following hierarchy of business purposes:

1. Generate paid projects.
2. Increase bargaining power.
3. Build trust.
4. Strengthen reputation and credibility.
5. Present a portfolio of work.
6. Communicate a commercial doctrine.
7. Function as a pitching asset.

Portfolio presentation is therefore a means to a commercial outcome, not the final outcome by itself.

## 2. Product doctrine

### 2.1 Owner doctrine

`CONFIRMED`

The product must follow this principle:

> Do not lead with the feature, workflow, tool, or technology. Lead with the valuable result that the buyer can understand. Technology supports the proof; it is not the primary product being sold.

Applied examples from the owner:

- B2B software should communicate the revenue opportunity, not only features.
- Automation should communicate time saved, not only workflow.
- AI tools should communicate completed work and production speed, not only the technology used.
- Buyers compare through logic but purchase when they understand the business impact.

### 2.2 Required messaging hierarchy

`INTERPRETATION_REQUIRES_CONFIRMATION`

Pages that sell GUSTI's work should normally communicate in this order:

1. Buyer problem or desired outcome.
2. Finished visual evidence.
3. Commercial or operational relevance.
4. Deliverables and scope.
5. Working method.
6. Tools or models used, when relevant.

Technology may be prominent inside Library reports, experiments, production notes, or technically relevant case-study sections. It must not replace the buyer outcome on Services or conversion-focused pages.

### 2.3 Evidence boundary

The doctrine does not authorize unverified revenue, conversion, sales, time-saving, ranking, or performance claims.

When financial or operational impact is not verified, the website may communicate only outcomes directly evidenced by the work, such as:

- A completed and visible deliverable.
- A documented output set.
- A verified production scope.
- A demonstrable content variation.
- A directly observed workflow characteristic.

Any stronger claim requires evidence defined in the future `CONTENT_EVIDENCE.md`.

## 5. Value proposition

### 5.1 Confirmed value direction

`CONFIRMED`

The website must sell results and completed work rather than relying on tools as the primary value proposition.

### 5.2 Proposed value structure

`INTERPRETATION_REQUIRES_CONFIRMATION`

GUSTI's value should be explained through four evidence-based dimensions:

1. **Commercial usefulness** — assets are presented in relation to a real product, campaign, marketplace, or selling context.
2. **Visible execution quality** — visitors can inspect the actual image, video, layout, or content output.
3. **Production range** — the portfolio can demonstrate multiple connected output types without presenting GUSTI as an unfocused generalist.
4. **Current applied knowledge** — Library reports and experiments demonstrate that GUSTI tests relevant releases and converts them into tangible output.

### 5.3 Prohibited value claims

The product must not publish the following without verified evidence:

- Revenue or sales increases.
- Conversion-rate increases.
- Time or cost savings.
- Global rankings or category leadership.
- Awards.
- Client satisfaction claims.
- ROI.
- Business transformation claims.
- Superlative quality claims that cannot be substantiated.

## 9. Content model and evidence governance

### 9.1 Core rule

`CONFIRMED`

Do not invent client facts, dates, roles, services, credits, outcomes, statistics, awards, prices, testimonials, or project narratives.

### 9.2 Required evidence states

The future `CONTENT_EVIDENCE.md` must define and apply at least these states:

| State | Meaning | Publish rule |
|---|---|---|
| `VERIFIED` | Supported by owner-provided evidence or an authoritative source | May publish within evidence scope |
| `CLIENT_REPORTED` | Reported by a client but not independently verified | May publish only with accurate attribution and permission |
| `OUTPUT_EVIDENCE_ONLY` | The visible output is real, but business impact is unknown | Publish the work; do not claim impact |
| `EXPERIMENTAL` | Personal or research experiment | Publish only with experimental labeling |
| `MISSING_DATA_REQUIRED` | Required information is absent | Do not invent; omit or visibly block publication |
| `FORBIDDEN_TO_PUBLISH` | Unverified, unauthorized, misleading, or owner-rejected content | Must not appear publicly |

### 9.3 Known current risks

`CURRENT_STATE`

The canonical repository currently includes:

- Generic fallback project details that generate dates, roles, services, and conversion claims.
- Project copy containing sales or performance claims that require evidence.
- External stock imagery used as project-detail fallback content.
- Work records with incomplete explicit detail data.

These are remediation dependencies, not approved product facts.

### 9.4 Localization integrity

- Translations must preserve factual meaning.
- Missing source content must not be expanded during translation.
- Claims must have the same evidence status in every language.
- Machine-generated translations require review before publication when they concern client facts, commercial claims, or contractual service information.

## 13. Accessibility requirements

The product must meet these baseline requirements:

- Every page has a semantic page-level heading.
- Interactive appearance always maps to an actual link, button, input, or defined action.
- Keyboard users can reach and operate all essential controls.
- Focus indicators are visible.
- Touch targets meet the minimum size defined in `docs/UI_GUIDELINES.md`.
- Color contrast meets WCAG AA for applicable text and controls.
- Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Modal focus is trapped and returned correctly.
- Media contains appropriate alternative text, captions, or labels based on content purpose.
- Language controls expose their current state.
- Browser Back, refresh, and direct navigation work for independent pages.

Formal accessibility conformance target beyond this baseline: `MISSING_DATA_REQUIRED`.
